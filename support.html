Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Left and right run. Up jumps, and a diagonal jump leaps that way. Down drops from hanging clothes. On a phone or tablet, drag the joystick and press the action button. A keyboard uses the arrow keys or A, D, W and S, and Space for the action.

How do I get into a room?

Climb a trash can, then the fence. Catch a garment and ride the clothesline. Jump or drop into an open window. All twelve windows open and close on their own. Bare rope will not hold Max.

What are the five rooms?

The Fishbowl, the Birdcage, the Big Cheese, the Flower Shelves, and Sleeping Dogs. Each visit picks one challenge and one of its two looks. Finish the room, return to the alley, and enter the window where Luna is calling.

What does a kiss do?

Climb the hearts to Luna. A kiss adds a life, up to nine, and the next round is a little harder. You start with three lives. Dogs, thrown rubbish, and a few room hazards cost a life. Falling on its own does not.

I ran out of adventures.

Free play gives five new adventures a day on your device. They return at midnight local time. Unlimited Play is an optional one-time purchase that removes the daily limit. If you already bought it on this Apple ID, use Restore purchase on the daily-limit screen.

Why does the alley look different at night?

Lighting follows the clock on your device: fog in the morning, bright sun at midday, golden light at sunset, and lamps after dark. Open the clock and pick Morning, Daytime, Sunset or Night if you want a different hour.

Do I need to save?

No. The game saves on its own. Progress stays on your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data resets it, and we are not able to recover it.

Can I change the language?

Yes. The game follows your device language and supports English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese, Korean, Italian, German, Vietnamese and Spanish. Change it any time in Settings.

Is there anything to buy?

The game is free to download, with five adventures a day. Unlimited Play is an optional one-time in-app purchase. There are no ads and no cash prizes.

The game feels slow or the picture will not start.

Alley Cat needs WebGL. Close other apps, restart the game, and open it in a current version of iOS. If the neighborhood still will not appear, email us with your device model.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad, in portrait and landscape. The desktop version is coming soon and is not available to download yet.
