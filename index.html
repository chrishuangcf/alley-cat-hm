How it plays

Climb. Ride. Leap. Find Luna.

Left and right run. Up jumps. Down drops from the clothes. A diagonal leap carries Max sideways. On a phone or tablet the joystick sits under your thumb, and the same moves work from a keyboard.

01

Climb

Jump onto a trash can, then up to the fence. Dogs and falling rubbish cost a life. A fall by itself does not.

02

Ride

Catch a garment on one of three clotheslines. It carries Max along. Bare rope will not hold him.

03

Leap

Jump between rows, or press down to drop. Twelve windows open and close on their own.

04

Find Luna

Finish a room and she calls from a glowing window. Climb the hearts. A kiss adds a life.

The back alley

A neighborhood that keeps your time.

The opening flies down a city street and into the alley, where Max waits on the fence. Start, and the camera settles into the classic side view. Portrait follows him along the building. Turn the device and the same adventure continues.

Morning brings fog. Noon is bright. Sunset warms the brick. After dark, the windows and the street lamp pool their light, and the rats catch a little glow so they never vanish into the wall.

Same alley, four lights

Set the hour, or let the clock decide.

Lighting follows the clock on your device, or a time you pick. These frames are the alley at morning, noon, sunset and night.

Where you are, it is … right now, so that is the light the game would use.

Behind the glass

A kiss, another life

Luna keeps the adventure going.

A finished room sends Max back outside, and one window glows. Inside, heart-shaped platforms lead up to her. A gift from the floor distracts a brother who gets too close. The kiss adds a life, up to nine, and the next round is a little harder.
